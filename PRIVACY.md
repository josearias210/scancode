# ScanCode Privacy Policy

Updated November 21, 2026

ScanCode is developed by Antonio Arias. For privacy questions, contact [josearias210@gmail.com](mailto:josearias210@gmail.com).

## Camera and photos

When you grant camera permission, ScanCode reads camera frames while the scanner is in use. The camera is optional. You can revoke access in Android settings and still scan photos. ScanCode accesses the image you choose in Android's photo picker. Camera frames and selected images are decoded on the device and are not stored by ScanCode or sent to the developer.

## Saved codes

When you tap **Save**, the decoded text, code format and scan time are kept in app-private storage on your device. Saved codes may include URLs, contact details, Wi-Fi credentials, or other information encoded in the original code. History is limited to the 200 most recent saved entries. ScanCode excludes its shared preferences from Android backup and device transfer.

History entries remain until you delete them, they fall outside the history limit, or you clear app data or uninstall the app. Open **History** to delete a code or select **Clear all** to delete the entire history. ScanCode has no user accounts.

## Actions you choose

**Copy** places the selected text in Android's clipboard. **Share** sends it to the app you pick through Android. Opening a link or another supported action hands the relevant content to the associated app. **Search the web** sends the query to Google Search through your browser. The receiving service or app handles that data under its own privacy terms.

## Barcode scanning SDK

ScanCode uses the bundled Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning SDK. Decoding works on the device without an internet connection. Google says that the SDK may collect diagnostic and usage information, including device and app metadata, a per-installation identifier, performance measurements, and SDK event data. Google encrypts this information in transit. ScanCode does not use ML Kit's automatic zoom feature. [ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure) · [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
