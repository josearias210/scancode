### Full description

ScanCode reads QR codes and barcodes from your camera or a photo. Scan a product label, check a link, or read a code from a screenshot.

- Scan live with tap-to-focus, zoom, three frame shapes, and torch support on compatible devices.
- Pick a photo from your device. When it has several codes, choose the one you want to read.
- Review the decoded value, then copy it, share it, or use the available action. Open links, prepare a call or message, and handle Wi-Fi codes from the result screen.
- Save the scans you want to use again. Search your local history or remove saved items when you're done.

The built-in barcode model works offline. ScanCode supports QR, UPC, EAN, Code 128, Data Matrix, PDF417, and other common barcode formats.

### What's new

A redesigned scanner with adjustable frames, multi-code photo scanning, clearer result actions, and searchable local history.
